Travelogue

This page has the campaign log for the Deathless D&D campaign. It's written in-universe by Lucy, with occasional corrections and insights from Nemah and the rest of the party (each in their own voice style).
Both the real-world session dates (plus unofficial names) and the in-game dates are included; note that the in-game dates represent when Lucy wrote in the journal (not necessarily the day the described events happened).
